# SyntaxLauncher privacy statement

The current application privacy statement is published at **https://syntaxdevteam.pl/p/syntaxlauncher-prywatnosc**. It covers local data, Microsoft/Xbox/Minecraft authentication and entitlement checks, native credential storage, network recipients, support correspondence, retention, deletion and children/guardian controls. Website cookies have a separate miniPORTAL notice linked there.

Publisher and direct privacy contact: **SyntaxDevTeam / WieszczY**, [wieszczy85@gmail.com](mailto:wieszczy85@gmail.com).

The inspected launcher has no built-in central analytics/advertising SDK or automatic crash-report upload. This does not describe the independent practices of Minecraft, mods or upstream services. Credentials and unreviewed game logs must not be sent to support. AppID approval and native/live account acceptance remain separate gates.
